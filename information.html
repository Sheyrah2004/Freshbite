Privacy

The booking form prepares a draft in your email app. Your details are sent to FreshBite only when you send that email. We use the name, email and visit details you provide to respond to your request. This website does not store booking details in your browser.

The embedded map is supplied by Google and subject to its privacy policy. No advertising cookies or external analytics collector are configured on this site.

For questions about information you’ve emailed to us, contact booking@freshbites.com.

Booking & menu

A booking request is not a confirmed reservation. Your table is reserved when FreshBite confirms availability by email. Opening hours are Monday–Friday, 08:00–22:00, East Africa Time.

Prices are in Ugandan shillings. Please check dish availability with our team. Tell us about allergies before ordering. Dietary labels are a guide; preparation areas may be shared. Food imagery is illustrative and presentation may vary.

Guest photos

Only share photos you have permission to use. We will check with you before publishing a guest photo. Ask our team for the current next-visit photo discount and gallery feature conditions.
